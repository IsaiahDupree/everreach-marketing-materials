## Overview

This document provides launch-ready creative briefs for the 5 most broadly relevant App Store Custom Product Pages (CPPs). ## CPP 1 — Sales: Keep Pipeline Warm

Increase installs from sales professionals by showing how EverReach prevents pipeline decay with Warmth Scores and AI follow-ups.

### Target Audience

AEs, SDRs, AMs, Partnerships, growth-minded sales pros.

### Promise

"Your pipeline stays warm automatically. Know exactly who to reach out to, and what to say."

### Keywords (Seed)

sales crm, relationship intelligence, pipeline warm, follow up reminder, ai email, sales assistant, account management, outreach personalization

- "3-minute routine that keeps deals moving."
- "AI writes follow-ups that sound like you."

## Cross-Creative Testing Plan

- Variables
  - Hook copy, first visual (warmth vs. AI vs. scan), CTA wording, proof line

- Budget & Cadence
  - Even split across 5 CPPs for first 7 days; kill losers on Day 4 using thresholds; scale top 2 creatives per CPP

- Kill/Keep Criteria
  - Kill: CTR < 0.6% and install rate < 1% after 1k impressions
  - Keep: CTR ≥ 1.0% or install rate ≥ 2%; promote variants

- KPIs
  - Acquisition: IPM/IPA, install rate, CPP CVR
  - Activation: Import rate, first AI message, first scan (scanner CPP)
  - Engagement: Weekly messages, reminders set, mobile opens

## Brand Specifications (From Brand Guidelines)

### Colors

**Primary Palette**:
- Black: `#000000` (Primary UI, buttons, text)
- White: `#FFFFFF` (Surfaces, cards)
- Background: `#F8F9FA` (Light mode)

**Warmth Score Colors** (Signature feature):
- Hot: `#FF6B6B` (Red - urgent priority)
- Warm: `#FFB366` (Orange - healthy)
- Cool: `#4ECDC4` (Teal - declining)
- Cold: `#95A5A6` (Gray - at risk)

**Accent Colors**:
- Success: `#10B981`
- Error: `#FF6B6B`
- Border: `#E5E5E5`
- Secondary Text: `#666666`

### Typography

- **Web**: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif
- **Mobile**: System fonts (San Francisco on iOS, Roboto on Android)
- **Headings**: Semi-bold to Bold (600-700 weight)
- **Body**: Regular (400 weight), 16px standard
- **UI Elements**: Semi-bold (600 weight), 16px

### Logo & Assets

- Located in: `/assets/images/`
- App Icon: `icon.png` (1024×1024)
- Use black logo on light backgrounds
- Use white logo on dark backgrounds
- Minimum clear space: 20% of logo height

## Asset Specs by Platform

### App Store (iOS)

- **Screenshots**: 6.7" iPhone (1290×2796), 6.5" (1242×2688); 6-10 images
- **Captions**: ≤ 30-45 characters ideal
- **Preview Video**: 15-30s; hook in first 3s; show in-app use
- **Icon**: 1024×1024 PNG

### All 9 Social Media Channels

**1. Facebook**
- Image Posts: 1200×630 (link preview)
- Video: 1080×1080 or 1920×1080
- Stories: 1080×1920 (9:16)
- Max video: 240 minutes (recommended: 15-90s)

**2. Instagram**
- Feed: 1080×1080 (1:1) or 1080×1350 (4:5)
- Stories: 1080×1920 (9:16)
- Reels: 1080×1920 (9:16), 15-90s
- Carousel: Up to 10 images

**3. Twitter/X**
- Image: 1200×675 (16:9)
- Video: 1280×720 or 720×1280
- Max video: 2:20 (recommended: 15-45s)

**4. LinkedIn**
- Image: 1200×627 (link preview)
- Video: 1920×1080 or 1080×1920
- Max video: 10 minutes (recommended: 30-90s)

**5. TikTok**
- Video: 1080×1920 (9:16)
- Duration: 15-60s (up to 10 min allowed)
- Hook in first 2 seconds critical

**6. YouTube**
- Video: 1920×1080 (16:9)
- Shorts: 1080×1920 (9:16), under 60s
- Thumbnails: 1280×720
- Titles: ≤ 60 characters

**7. Pinterest**
- Standard Pin: 1000×1500 (2:3)
- Square: 1000×1000 (1:1)
- Video: 1080×1920 (9:16), 6-15s ideal

**8. Reddit**
- Image: 1200×628 recommended
- Video: varies by subreddit
- Approach: Educational/helpful, not promotional

**9. WhatsApp/Telegram** (Communities)
- Image: 1080×1080 or 1200×630
- Video: 1080×1920 (vertical) or 1920×1080
- Max size: 16MB (WhatsApp), 2GB (Telegram)

### General Video Specs

- Format: MP4 (H.264)
- Frame rate: 30fps (60fps for action)
- Bitrate: 8-12 Mbps
- Audio: AAC, 128 kbps minimum
- Captions: Always include (80% watch without sound)

## Tracking & UTM Parameters

**Standard Structure**:
```
utm_source={facebook|instagram|twitter|linkedin|tiktok|youtube|pinterest|reddit}
utm_medium=cpc|organic|social
utm_campaign=cpp_{theme}_q4_2025
utm_content={hook_variant}_{format}
utm_term={keyword} (for paid search)
```

**Examples**:
- Facebook Ad: `?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=cpp_sales_q4_2025&utm_content=pipeline_cold_video_15s`
- Organic TikTok: `?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=cpp_scanner_q4_2025&utm_content=card_scan_demo`
