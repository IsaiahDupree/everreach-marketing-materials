- **Encryption**: AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit

- **Monitoring**: 24/7 security monitoring and alerts
- **Backups**: Encrypted, automated daily backups
- **Audits**: Regular security assessments and penetration testing

### 5.2 Data Centers

- Physical security controls
- Redundant infrastructure
- 99.9% uptime SLA
- Disaster recovery procedures

### 5.3 Your Responsibility

- Maintaining strong passwords

While we use reasonable efforts to protect your information, no security system is impenetrable. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 14. Specific Privacy Practices

### 14.1 Email Integration

If you connect your email:
- We access only necessary metadata (sender, recipient, date)
- We do not read email content unless you explicitly enable that feature
- You can disconnect email anytime
- We do not store full email bodies (only metadata)

### 14.2 Contact Import

When you import contacts:
- Data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- We only import contacts you select
- You can delete imported contacts anytime
- We do not access your full address book without permission

### 14.3 Screenshot Analysis

When you upload screenshots:
- Images are processed by AI (GPT-4 Vision)
- Extracted text is stored
- Original images are deleted after processing (unless you save them)
- You control what data is extracted

### 14.4 Voice Notes

When you record voice notes:
- Audio is transcribed using AI (Whisper)
- Transcriptions are stored
- Original audio can be deleted automatically
- You can disable voice note features

### 14.5 AI Message Generation

When you use AI message composer:
- We send contact context to OpenAI
- Data is encrypted in transit
- OpenAI does not use this data for training
- You review all AI-generated content before sending
