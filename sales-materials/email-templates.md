## Best Practices

### Email Timing
- **Best days**: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- **Best times**: 10 AM - 11 AM, 2 PM - 3 PM (recipient's timezone)
- **Avoid**: Monday mornings, Friday afternoons, weekends

### Follow-Up Cadence
- **Day 0**: Initial outreach
- **Day 3**: First follow-up
- **Day 7**: Second follow-up
- **Day 14**: Final follow-up
- **Day 30**: Re-engagement attempt

### Subject Line Tips
- Keep under 50 characters
- Personalize when possible
- Avoid spam triggers ("FREE!", "ACT NOW!")
- Questions perform well
- Create curiosity, not confusion

### Email Body Best Practices
- Keep under 150 words for cold emails
- Use white space (short paragraphs)
- One clear CTA per email
- Always personalize {{FirstName}} minimum
- End with a question when possible

### A/B Testing Ideas
- Subject line variations
- CTA button text
- Email length (short vs. long)
- Social proof placement
- Personal tone vs. professional tone
