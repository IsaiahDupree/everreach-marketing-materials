# Sales Call Scripts - EverReach CRM

## Discovery Call Scripts

### Script 1: Founder/CEO Discovery Call

**Opening (1-2 minutes)**

"Hi {{FirstName}}, thanks for taking the time to chat. Before I tell you about EverReach, I'd love to learn more about how you're currently managing your professional relationships.

Can you walk me through your current process for staying in touch with investors, partners, and key contacts?"

**Discovery Questions (5-10 minutes)**

1. **Network Size**
   - "How many professional contacts would you say you have? (LinkedIn, phone, email combined)"
   - "Of those, how many do you actively stay in touch with?"

2. **Current System**
   - "How are you tracking these relationships today? Spreadsheet? CRM? Memory?"
   - "What's working well about your current system?"
   - "What's frustrating about it?"

3. **Pain Points**
   - "When was the last time you forgot to follow up with someone important?"
   - "How much time per week do you spend trying to manage your network?"
   - "Have you ever lost an opportunity because a relationship went cold?"

4. **Specific Use Cases**
   - **For Fundraising**: "How do you track investor relationships between funding rounds?"
   - **For Partnerships**: "How do you maintain relationships with potential partners?"
   - **For Customers**: "How do you stay top-of-mind with key customers?"

5. **Decision Criteria**
   - "If you could wave a magic wand, what would the perfect relationship management solution look like?"
   - "What would need to be true for you to make a change from your current system?"

**Transition to Demo (1 minute)**

"Got it. Based on what you've shared, I think EverReach could be really helpful for you, specifically around [their pain point]. Let me show you how it works and you can tell me if I'm off base."

## Best Practices

### General Call Tips
- Always use the prospect's name
- Reference specific details about their business
- Ask permission to ask questions
- Listen more than you talk (70/30 rule)
- Take notes during the call
- Confirm next steps before hanging up

### Discovery Best Practices
- Start with open-ended questions
- Dig deeper with "Tell me more about that"
- Let silence work for you (don't fill every gap)
- Uncover pain before presenting solution
- Understand their buying process early

### Demo Best Practices
- Show, don't tell
- Use their specific use case
- Keep it short (15-20 minutes max)
- Get micro-commitments throughout
- End with clear next steps

### Closing Best Practices
- Ask for the sale directly
- Create urgency without being pushy
- Use trial closes throughout the call
- Address objections head-on
- Always have a follow-up plan

### Follow-Up Best Practices
- Call within 24 hours of demo
- Reference specific points from previous call
- Add value with each touchpoint
- Use multiple channels (email, phone, LinkedIn)
- Know when to walk away
