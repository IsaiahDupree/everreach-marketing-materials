## Production Notes

### Video Formats to Export

1. **Vertical (1080×1920)**: Instagram Stories/Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Snapchat
2. **Horizontal (1920×1080)**: YouTube, LinkedIn, Facebook feed
3. **Square (1080×1080)**: Instagram feed, LinkedIn feed, Facebook feed

### Technical Specs

- **Frame Rate**: 30fps (60fps if showing fast motion)
- **Bitrate**: 8-12 Mbps
- **Audio**: AAC, 128 kbps minimum
- **Duration markers**: Exact timing for each scene
- **Safe zones**: Keep text/logo within 10% margin from edges

### Captions Requirements

- **Always include** (80% watch without sound)
- **Font**: Arial Bold or similar sans-serif
- **Size**: Readable on mobile (minimum 48pt for 1080 height)
- **Color**: White text with black stroke/shadow for readability
- **Position**: Lower third for horizontal, middle-to-upper for vertical

### Music/SFX

- **Royalty-free** music only (Epidemic Sound, Artlist, or similar)
- **Volume**: -18 to -20 dB (don't overpower voice-over)
- **Transitions**: Subtle whooshes for scene changes
- **UI sounds**: Light taps, notification pings (optional, keep minimal)

### Branding

- **Logo**: Appears in last 3-5 seconds
- **Colors**: Use brand palette (Black `#000000`, Warmth colors for scores)
- **CTA**: Clear, contrasting button in final frame
- **Website**: Always show everreach.app in lower third

### A/B Test Variants

For each script, create variations testing:
- **Hook**: First 3 seconds (test 2-3 different opens)
- **Feature order**: Warmth Score first vs. AI Composer first
- **CTA wording**: "Install Now" vs. "Try Free" vs. "Start Free Trial"
- **Proof**: With metrics vs. without (once real data available)

### Approval Checklist

- [ ] Scripts reviewed and approved
- [ ] Storyboards created
- [ ] App UI screens captured (light mode)
- [ ] Voice-over recorded (professional or AI voice)
- [ ] Music licensed
- [ ] Captions timed and tested
- [ ] Brand guidelines followed
- [ ] All format variations exported
- [ ] File naming: `everreach-cpp{1|2}-{platform}-{duration}s-v{1|2}.mp4`
