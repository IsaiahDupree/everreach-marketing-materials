**Kill Criteria** (after 1,000 impressions):
- CTR < 0.8%
- CPI > $5.00 (adjust based on LTV)
- Install rate < 1%

**Scale Criteria**:
- CTR > 1.5%
- CPI < $2.50
- Install rate > 2.5%

## Risk Mitigation

### Potential Issues & Solutions

**Issue**: Ad approval delays
- **Solution**: Submit ads 48h before launch, have backup variants ready

**Issue**: App Store screenshot rejection
- **Solution**: Follow Apple guidelines exactly, avoid overl ay text >30% of screen

**Issue**: High CPI
- **Solution**: Pause campaigns, test new creatives, adjust targeting

**Issue**: Low conversion rate
- **Solution**: A/B test landing pages, simplify onboarding, improve messaging

**Issue**: Attribution tracking broken
- **Solution**: Test tracking before launch, have backup manual tracking
