## Brand Voice & Messaging

### Brand Personality

**EverReach is**:
- Professional but approachable
- Confident but not arrogant
- Helpful without being pushy
- Smart without being condescending
- Modern and tech-forward

**EverReach is NOT**:
- Overly casual or unprofessional
- Aggressive or sales-y
- Jargon-heavy or complex
- Cold or corporate
- Gimmicky or trendy

### Tone Guidelines

**Do's**:
- Use "you" and "your" (speak directly to user)
- Lead with benefits, not features
- Be specific with numbers and examples
- Use active voice
- Ask questions to engage
- Show empathy for pain points

**Don'ts**:
- Use unnecessary jargon

## Best Practices

### For Marketing Campaigns

**Landing Pages**:
- Keep above-the-fold content focused
- Use one primary CTA per page
- Include social proof early
- Optimize for mobile
- Load speed under 3 seconds
- A/B test headlines

**Email Campaigns**:
- Subject lines under 50 characters
- Preview text optimized
- Single, clear CTA
- Mobile-responsive design
- Personalization beyond first name
- Unsubscribe link prominent

**Ad Campaigns**:
- Start with small budgets ($20-50/day)
- Test multiple variations
- Target specific audience segments
- Track conversions, not just clicks
- Retarget website visitors
- Optimize based on data

### For Sales Outreach

**Cold Emails**:
- Personalize the first line
- Lead with their problem, not your solution
- Keep under 150 words
- One clear ask
- Follow up 3-4 times
- Track open and click rates

**Discovery Calls**:
- Research prospect beforehand
- Ask more than you talk (70/30 rule)
- Listen for pain points
- Take detailed notes
- Confirm next steps before ending
- Follow up within 24 hours

**Demos**:
- Customize to their use case
- Show, don't tell
- Address objections proactively
- Keep under 20 minutes
- Get micro-commitments throughout
- End with clear next action

### For Legal Compliance

**Website**:
- Terms and Privacy links in footer
- Cookie consent banner (GDPR)
- Privacy policy easily accessible
- Clear data collection disclosure
- Unsubscribe options

**Email**:
- CAN-SPAM compliant unsubscribe
- Physical address in footer
- No deceptive subject lines
- Honor opt-outs within 10 days

**Data**:
- Encrypt sensitive data
- Regular backups
- Access controls
- Data retention policies
- Breach notification procedures
