# Launch Email Campaign — Subject Lines

EverReach Marketing | 2025-11-06

---

## Overview

This document contains 7 email campaigns designed to build anticipation, announce the launch, and drive downloads for EverReach. Each campaign includes multiple subject line options following different copywriting styles (Cosmo Concept, Darlene + Clark, Wait! What?).

---

## Campaign 1: Tease / Pre-Launch Hype

**Goal**: Build curiosity and anticipation.

**Subject Line Options**:

1. You've never connected like this before. → (Cosmo Concept – intrigue & promise)
2. wait till you see what we've been building… → (Darlene + Clark – casual, conversational tone)
3. Your contacts are about to get closer. → (Cosmo Concept – subtle emotional hook)

**Recommended**: Option 1 for professional audience, Option 2 for personal relationships audience

**Body Copy Guidelines**:
- Don't reveal too much—tease the problem EverReach solves
- Include date countdown ("3 days until...")
- End with waitlist CTA or "Stay tuned"

---

## Campaign 2: The Big Reveal

**Goal**: Announce EverReach with clarity and excitement.

**Subject Line Options**:

1. It's here: Meet EverReach → (Clear & celebratory)
2. Connection, upgraded. → (Short, modern, sleek)
3. wait… EverReach? yep. → (Darlene + Clark casual "friend text" vibe)

**Recommended**: Option 1 for launch day announcement

**Body Copy Guidelines**:
- Lead with the hero value prop: "Never lose touch with the people who matter"
- Include Warmth Score explanation (simple)
- Clear download CTA with App Store/Play Store links
- Include hero screenshot (Dashboard with Warmth Scores)

---

## Campaign 3: Why It Matters / Emotional Hook

**Goal**: Make the user feel the emotional value of connection.

**Subject Line Options**:

1. Because staying close shouldn't be complicated. → (Cosmo Concept – relatable simplicity)
2. What if you never lost touch again? → (Curiosity + emotion)
3. thought about you — and how EverReach helps you do the same → (Darlene + Clark – intimate tone)

**Recommended**: Option 2 for broad appeal

**Body Copy Guidelines**:
- Open with relatable pain point: "When was the last time you talked to [old friend]?"
- Show how relationships naturally drift
- Position EverReach as the solution that remembers for you
- Include testimonial or emotional story
- CTA: "Download now and reconnect"

---

## Campaign 4: Social Proof / Story

**Goal**: Share early users' excitement or behind-the-scenes energy.

**Subject Line Options**:

1. "I didn't realize how disconnected I was until this." → (Wait! What? – curiosity via quote)
2. how 237 early users are reconnecting already → (Social proof + data hook)
3. we built EverReach for moments like this. → (Warm, storytelling tone)

**Recommended**: Option 1 for high curiosity, Option 2 for credibility

**Body Copy Guidelines**:
- Lead with user testimonial or story
- Include specific results: "Sent 43 messages in first week"
- Share founder story (why we built this)
- Show community momentum
- CTA: "Join the movement"

---

## Campaign 5: Call to Action / Invitation

**Goal**: Get them to download the app.

**Subject Line Options**:

1. Join the connection revolution (before your friends do) → (Cosmo Concept – FOMO + energy)
2. your invite to EverReach is waiting → (Personal invitation tone)
3. don't be the last to reconnect → (Conversational urgency)

**Recommended**: Option 2 for warmth, Option 1 for urgency

**Body Copy Guidelines**:
- Create sense of exclusivity or invitation
- Highlight unique features: AI messages, Warmth Scores
- Include limited-time offer (if applicable): "Free trial extended"
- Make download process seem easy: "3 taps to better relationships"
- Prominent CTA button: "Get EverReach Now"

---

## Campaign 6: Reminder / Gentle Nudge

**Goal**: Reignite interest among non-openers or non-downloaders.

**Subject Line Options**:

1. Still thinking about it? EverReach isn't. → (Playful reversal)
2. hey, you in? → (Darlene + Clark minimalism – feels like a text)
3. one tap away from better conversations. → (Benefit-forward clarity)

**Recommended**: Option 2 for casual warmth, Option 3 for clarity

**Body Copy Guidelines**:
- Acknowledge hesitation: "Not sure if EverReach is for you?"
- Address common objections: "Takes 5 min to set up, saves hours of guilt"
- Show simplicity: "Just import contacts and go"
- Offer help: "Questions? Reply to this email"
- Softer CTA: "Give it a try" or "See for yourself"

---

**Goal**: Thank users, reinforce brand warmth, and build ongoing engagement.

**Subject Line Options**:

2. Connected yet? Here's what's next for our community → (Future-focused & inclusive)

**Body Copy Guidelines**:

## A/B Testing Recommendations

### Test 1: Tone
- **A**: Formal/professional (Cosmo Concept style)
- **B**: Casual/conversational (Darlene + Clark style)
- **Measure**: Open rate, click rate

### Test 2: Length
- **A**: Short subject (under 30 chars): "hey, you in?"
- **B**: Longer subject (40-60 chars): "Your contacts are about to get closer."
- **Measure**: Open rate

### Test 3: Punctuation
- **A**: Period ending: "Connection, upgraded."
- **B**: Arrow: "Connection, upgraded →"
- **C**: No punctuation: "Connection upgraded"
- **Measure**: Open rate

### Test 4: Personalization
- **A**: Generic: "Join the connection revolution"
- **B**: Personalized: "{{FirstName}}, join the connection revolution"
- **Measure**: Open rate, click rate

---

## Subject Line Best Practices

### DO:
- Keep under 60 characters (mobile truncation)
- Use action words: join, discover, meet, reconnect
- Create curiosity without clickbait
- Test personalization (first name)
- A/B test emoji usage (✨, 🎉, ❤️)
- Use lowercase for casual tone (when appropriate)

### DON'T:
- Use all caps (SPAM filters)
- Overuse punctuation (!!!)
- Make false promises
- Use misleading hooks
- Ignore mobile preview text
- Forget to A/B test

---

## Platform-Specific Considerations

### Email Provider Optimizations

**Gmail**:
- Keep subject under 50 chars (mobile truncation earlier)
- Use preview text effectively (first 90 chars of body)
- Avoid spam trigger words: "free", "limited time", "act now"

**Apple Mail**:
- Emoji display consistently
- Preview text shows more (up to 140 chars)
- Dark mode compatibility important

**Outlook**:
- Conservative with emoji
- Formal tone may perform better
- Subject truncates at 50 chars

## Performance Benchmarks

### Target Metrics (App Launch Emails)

**Open Rate**:
- Pre-launch tease: 25-35%
- Launch announcement: 35-50%
- Follow-up campaigns: 20-30%

**Click Rate**:
- Launch announcement: 8-15%
- Call to action: 10-18%
- Reminder: 5-10%

**Conversion Rate** (email → download):
- Launch week: 5-12%
- Post-launch: 3-8%

### Kill Criteria (After 1,000 sends):
- Open rate < 15%
- Click rate < 3%
- Unsubscribe rate > 1%

### Scale Criteria:
- Open rate > 35%
- Click rate > 12%
- Conversion rate > 8%

## Copy Style Guide

### Cosmo Concept Style
**Characteristics**:
- Aspirational, confident
- Clear value propositions
- Emotional hooks, subtle sophistication
- Modern, polished

**Examples**:
- "You've never connected like this before."
- "Because staying close shouldn't be complicated."
- "Join the connection revolution (before your friends do)"

### Darlene + Clark Style
**Characteristics**:
- Casual, conversational
- Feels like a text from a friend
- Lowercase, relaxed punctuation
- Intimate, warm

**Examples**:
- "wait till you see what we've been building…"
- "wait… EverReach? yep."
- "hey, you in?"

### Wait! What? Style
**Characteristics**:
- Curiosity-driven
- Pattern interrupt
- Quotations, surprising stats
- Immediate intrigue

**Examples**:
- "I didn't realize how disconnected I was until this."
- "Still thinking about it? EverReach isn't."

## Localization Notes

### Tone Adjustments by Region

**US**: Casual, aspirational, FOMO-driven works well
**UK**: Slightly more formal, less hyperbolic

**Canada**: Balanced, polite, community-focused

### Cultural Considerations

- Avoid US-centric holidays in international campaigns
- Test emoji usage by region (more conservative in Europe)
- Consider time zones for send times
- Localize currency mentions, if applicable
