## Best Practices for LinkedIn Ads

### Targeting Recommendations

**Job Titles to Target**:
- Founders / CEO / Co-Founder
- VP of Sales / Sales Director
- Consultant / Strategy Consultant
- Business Development Manager
- Account Executive / Account Manager
- Partner / Managing Partner
- Executive / C-Suite

**Industries to Target**:
- Technology / Software
- Professional Services
- Financial Services
- Consulting
- Real Estate
- Marketing / Advertising

**Company Size**:
- 1-10 (Startups/Solo)
- 11-50 (Small Business)
- 51-200 (Mid-Market)

**Seniority Levels**:
- Director
- VP
- C-Level
- Owner

### Content Strategy

**Cold Traffic**:
- Focus on problem awareness
- Use pain point messaging
- Include social proof
- Offer free trials, not demos

**Warm Traffic**:
- Feature-focused content
- Comparison to alternatives
- Detailed testimonials
- Stronger CTAs

**Retargeting**:
- Address objections directly
- Show ROI calculations
- Create urgency (trial ending)
- Offer time-limited deals

### Performance Benchmarks

**Expected Metrics**:
- CTR: 0.5-1.5% (Sponsored Content)
- CPC: $8-15
- Conversion Rate: 2-5%
- CAC: $150-300
- LTV: $500-1,000+ (assuming 2+ year retention)

**Optimization Tips**:
- A/B test every element
- Refresh creatives monthly
- Adjust bids for high-performers
- Exclude converting audiences from cold campaigns
- Use matched audiences for retargeting
