## Platform-Specific Best Practices

### Facebook Feed Ads
- **Image**: High contrast, minimal text
- **Video**: Hook in first 3 seconds
- **Copy**: Front-load value proposition
- **CTA**: "Start Free Trial" performs best

### Instagram Feed Ads
- **Image**: Aesthetic, lifestyle-focused
- **Copy**: Shorter, emoji-friendly
- **CTA**: "Learn More" for cold traffic

### Instagram Stories
- **Design**: Vertical, mobile-optimized
- **Text**: Large, readable fonts
- **CTA**: Swipe-up to landing page
- **Duration**: 5-7 seconds per frame

### Facebook/Instagram Reels
- **Length**: 15-30 seconds optimal
- **Sound**: Trending audio increases reach
- **Captions**: Always include (80% watch muted)
- **Hook**: First 3 seconds critical

### Messenger Ads
- **Tone**: Conversational, friendly
- **Response Time**: Under 1 minute ideal
- **Buttons**: Max 3 options per message
- **Goal**: Move to trial signup within 5 messages

## A/B Testing Recommendations

### Test These Variables

**Headlines**:
- Problem-focused vs. Solution-focused
- Question vs. Statement
- Benefit vs. Feature

**Images**:
- Product screenshots vs. Lifestyle images
- People vs. UI
- Before/After vs. Single frame

**Copy Length**:
- Short (2-3 sentences) vs. Long (150+ words)

**CTAs**:
- "Start Free Trial" vs. "Try It Free"
- "Learn More" vs. "Get Started"
- "Sign Up" vs. "Join Free"

**Audience**:
- Lookalike vs. Interest-based
- Retargeting vs. Cold traffic
- Age ranges (25-34, 35-44, 45-54)

### Winning Combinations (Based on SaaS benchmarks)
- Problem + Social proof + Free trial CTA
- Feature demo + Time-saving benefit + "Try It Free"
- Before/After + Transformation story + "Start Free"
